## A — The Mentor

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: polished brand mascot concept model sheet for an existing professional Smart Clipboard & Visual Workspace product, for the owner to choose a visual direction. This is a concept sheet, not a website.
Primary request: a WHITE SNOWY WOLF mascot, unmistakably a wolf with upright triangular ears, a tapered wolf muzzle, fluffy cheek ruff and a bushy white tail. Anthropomorphic upright posture. Wears eyeglasses and a complete tailored suit, white shirt, and tie, and HOLDS A TEACHING POINTER STICK. Intelligent, professional, friendly, trustworthy, welcoming, feels like a teacher guiding people. Warm closed-mouth smile, no intimidating teeth. Adult professional, not a baby or a dog.
Composition: a spacious landscape model sheet with exactly SIX separate full-body depictions of THE SAME CHARACTER, aligned in a clean 3 columns x 2 rows grid, all at consistent scale, no overlapping limbs, entire ears, shoes, tails and pointer fully within frame. Top row: front view, true side profile, three-quarter view. Bottom row: pointing/teaching with raised pointer, relaxed standing with pointer resting downward, explaining with open free palm and pointer in other hand. White or very pale neutral background, tiny unobtrusive ground shadows, ample empty gutters. Each depiction must preserve exactly the same face, glasses, suit, fur markings, proportions, and accessories. Six depictions, no additional characters.
Text: only a discreet short concept title at top and small labels beneath each figure reading "Front", "Side", "3/4", "Teaching", "Standing", "Explaining". Clean readable sans-serif typography.
Avoid: website UI, product screenshots, classroom chalkboard, scenery, props other than pointer, watermark, logos, aggressive expression, photorealistic animal, crowded layout, inconsistent anatomy or costume.
Concept title: "A — THE MENTOR". Style: premium contemporary 2D character illustration, restrained vector-like shapes with subtle dimensional shading and elegant fine contour lines. Distinctive but approachable wolf face, normal adult proportions slightly stylized, slim charcoal eyeglass frames, a neat deep charcoal suit with a subtle cool undertone. A calm, assured, practical expert. Most scalable and versatile direction for software branding; polished editorial craft.
